Century 21 teamed up with Hornet Directors Natalie Labarre and Yves Geleyn to celebrate their 50th-anniversary with 50 years of real-life realtor stories. Across five films—and dozens of illustrations—Natalie and Yves bring an array of personal anecdotes to visual life, ranging from the surreal to the sentimental.
