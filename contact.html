I received my BA in Animation from Beijing Institute of Fashion Technology and an MFA in Digital Arts from Pratt Institute. Following graduation, I worked as an animator in New York, collaborating with clients including Warner Bros., McDonald’s, Facebook, and Instagram. Alongside my commercial practice, I have continued my theoretical studies and research. After developing my own conceptual framework, I transitioned to fine art, translating these evolving ideas into the tangible, haptic materiality of oil painting.
